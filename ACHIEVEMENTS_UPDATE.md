# Achievement System Update

Hunger Fish now includes 77 reachable achievements covering feeding, bosses, progression, growth, combos, score, survival, zone mechanics, hazards, pickups, economy, skins, and cumulative damage.

## Rewards
Every achievement has a one-time coin reward. Rewards are granted automatically when the achievement unlocks and are persisted in localStorage.

## Tracking
Persistent tracking includes fish eaten, bosses defeated, games played, wins, perfect wins, best score, best combo, best player size, hazard dodges, serpent defeats, toxic immunity, boosters collected, health boosters, coins earned/spent, owned skins, damage taken, and completed levels.

## UI
The Achievements page now shows all achievements with descriptions and coin rewards. It supports mouse-wheel scrolling so the full catalogue can be viewed. A toast notification appears whenever an achievement is unlocked.
